This site uses [Astro Sienna](https://astro.build/themes/details/astro-sienna/), a theme designed by [Anjay Goel](https://anjay.sh/).

Among all the themes I explored while looking for a way to bring my personal website into existence, *Astro Sienna* is the one I liked the most. I am genuinely happy to have found a theme that feels so visually coherent while remaining simple and understandable. The color scheme, the little animations, the typography, the overall visual appeal, and the simplicity of the codebase all appealed to me.

I really appreciate Anjay for creating something like this and publishing it under the MIT License.

However, I wanted some modifications. Since I am not a web-development person, these were generously made by a friend of mine, [Ayush Soni](https://github.com/Ayush-soni-12/). There are some easy ones, and I made them myself.
